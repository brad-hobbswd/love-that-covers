The heart behind the book

Love does not ignore the wound. It covers it with grace.

Love That Covers explores the places where hurt, disappointment, forgiveness, family, faith, and healing meet.

This is not a story about pretending pain never happened. It is an invitation to discover what Jesus can do with the places we thought could never be restored.
